Prototype Deliverables

Shortlisted teams are required to build and demonstrate a working prototype at the venue.

1. Functional Prototype

Working software application, AI pipeline, IoT edge node, or hardware prototype built during the 36 hours.

2. Code Repository

Clean GitHub / GitLab repository containing complete source code, hardware schematics (if applicable), and documentation.

3. Live Jury Demonstration

Live demonstration of your working solution, architecture explanation, and Q&A defense with the expert jury.

Code of Conduct & Venue Perks

Hackathon Guidelines & Hospitality

Important information for participants at Manipal University Jaipur.

Originality & Ethics

All prototypes must be built during the hackathon. Plagiarism, pre-built turnkey commercial solutions, or code theft will result in disqualification.

Hospitality & Rest Rooms

Common Rest Rooms are provided at MUJ, one for boys and one for girls with no additional charges.

Meals & Refreshments

Wholesome meals and refreshments are provided to all teams throughout the 36-hour sprint.

Jury Evaluation

Judges evaluate based on Originality (30%), Technical Innovation (25%), Practical Feasibility (25%), and Real-world Impact (20%).
