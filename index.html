About The Host

Manipal University Jaipur & IIC 3.0

The International Innovation Challenge (IIC) is hosted by the Department of IoT & Intelligent Systems, School of Computer Science and Engineering, Manipal University Jaipur, to empower young minds to solve real-world problems through hands-on innovation.

The Manipal Education and Medical Group is an established leader in education and research. Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ) launched in 2011 on an invitation from the Government of Rajasthan, redefining academic excellence with practical, experiential learning.

Submission Format

PPT Submission Guidelines

Teams must submit their project in the form of a PPT of up to 9 slides (as per provided template).

Official 9-Slide Structure

Slide 1: Team & TitleProject name, theme, team members & college.
Slide 2: Problem StatementClear definition of the problem addressed.
Slide 3: Proposed SolutionCore innovation & unique value proposition.
Slide 4: Technical ArchitectureHardware, AI models & software stack.
Slide 5: FeasibilityPractical implementation & viability.
Slide 6: Impact & ScopeSocial, economic, or industrial significance.
Slide 7: Prototype (Optional)GitHub code repo or demo video link.
Slide 8: Future RoadmapNext steps & commercial deployment.
Slide 9: ConclusionSummary of takeaways and references.
